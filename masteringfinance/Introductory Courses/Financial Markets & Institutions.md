# Financial Markets & Institutions

This course will cover capital flows: stock exchanges, bond markets, commercial banks, investment banks, mutual funds, and central banks.

---

## Core

**Financial Markets** _/ Yale, Coursera_ [Course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/financial-markets-global)

- Free, Taught by Nobel Laureate Robert Shiller, this course is considered one of the best introductory courses to learning about financial systems, behavioral finance, and how markets impact society.

**Financial Markets and Institutions, Global Edition** _/ Frederic Mishkin, Stanley Eakins_ [Book ~$50](https://www.amazon.com/Financial-Markets-Institutions-Frederic-Mishkin/dp/1292459549/)

- University textbook for understanding the Federal Reserve, banking regulations, and a much more deeper dive into how financial markets work.

---

## Supplementary

**Federal Reserve Education** [Website](https://www.federalreserveeducation.org/)

- Official educational branch of the United States Federal Reserve. Informative website to learn about monetary policy, inflation, and interest rates.
