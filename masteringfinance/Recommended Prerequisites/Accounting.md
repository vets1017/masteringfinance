# Accounting

Accounting is what many considers the foundational language of business. You cannot value a company, model, or pitch a stock if you cannot read its financial statements. Accounting allows you to understand ones financial health. 

---
## Core

**Financial Accounting** */ University of Pennsylvania, Coursera [Course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/wharton-accounting)
- Free to audit. Teaches the basics of how to read, understand, and analyze financial statements. This course covers balance sheet, income statement, cash flows, and ratios. 
**Financial Accounting** & **Managerial Accounting** */ Tony Bell, YouTube*
- Excellent videos produced by Tony Bell that covers Financial Accounting and Managerial Accounting. These playlists cover how to use financial accounting to report on a company's financial health and profitability and use management accounting for budgeting, forecasting, and day-to-day decision-making.
