# Personal Finance & Literacy

Foundation of money management. This is usually considered core foundational knowledge and a highly recommended prerequisite before entering the world of finance. You must understand how to manage your own wallet before you manage a company's balance sheet.

This course will cover: Budgeting, Credit, Taxes, and Basic Investing.

--- 
## Core

**Personal Finance** */ Khan Academy, 2017*  [Course](https://www.khanacademy.org/college-careers-more/personal-finance)
- Free, beginner-friendly financial education provided by Khan Academy. This course covers basic personal finance concepts such as budgeting, investments, housing, taxes, and many other useful topics. 

> [!NOTE]
This course hasn't been updated since 2017.

**Financial Literacy** */ Khan Academy* [Course](https://www.khanacademy.org/college-careers-more/financial-literacy)
- Another free, beginner-friendly financial literacy course provided by Khan Academy. Unlike their [Personal finance course](https://www.khanacademy.org/college-careers-more/personal-finance), this one is updated constantly and goes more in-depth about certain topics. This course covers good financial practices, common scams and fraud, careers, and many other useful topics.

## Supplementary

**r/personalfinance** [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinance/)
- An active Reddit community that has its own wiki that is regularly updated. Check out their [Prime Directive](https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinance/wiki/commontopics/), a community-built wiki / [flowchart](https://imgur.com/personal-income-spending-flowchart-united-states-lSoUQr2) that tells you what to do with your money, step by step.

**Financial Planning Tools** */ Investor.gov, U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission* [Website](https://www.investor.gov/free-financial-planning-tools)
- A set of free financial planning tools that are convenient to know and have.
